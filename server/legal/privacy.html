Privacy Policy

Sisyphusball · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Sisyphusball is a game in which you push a stone up a mountain that has no summit. This policy explains what little information the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Your Pi username and Pi user IDPi Network, when you sign in with the Pi SDK (scopes: username, payments)To show your name on the Hall of Fame and keep your records together
Guest nameYou, when you choose Play as GuestSame as above, for players outside the Pi Browser
Scores and play historyThe gameBest height, number of climbs, total meters pushed, and when you last played — used for the ranking
Current height while climbingThe game, about once per secondTo show other players who is climbing right now

We do not collect your email address, phone number, wallet address, location, contacts, or payment information. Sisyphusball has no in-app purchases and never asks you to pay. The payments scope only lets us send you Pi (for example a small thank-you to testers); such a transfer goes through Pi Network to your Pi wallet, and we keep only its payment ID.

What other players can see

Your username (or guest name), your best height, your title and your number of climbs appear on the public Hall of Fame. While you are climbing, your username and current height are shown to other players in real time.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. If you play as a guest, your chosen name is stored in your browser's local storage so you do not have to type it again. Your sign-in session is kept in memory only and ends when you close the game.

How your Pi sign-in works

When you sign in, the Pi Browser gives the game a temporary access token. Our server sends that token to the Pi Network API only to confirm your username and user ID. We do not store the token.

Where the data is kept

Records are stored on our game server, hosted on Microsoft Azure. Server logs may briefly record a username when a personal best is set. We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

How long we keep it

Records are kept for as long as the game runs, so the Hall of Fame stays meaningful (or appears to). You can ask us to delete your record at any time.

Your choices

  • To have your record removed from the Hall of Fame and deleted, contact us with your Pi username.
  • To remove a stored guest name, clear this site's data in your browser.

Children

Sisyphusball is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
